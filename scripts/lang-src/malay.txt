# Malay (Bahasa Melayu) — authored source for data/langs/malay.json
# Latin script, LTR. Spoken mainly in Malaysia, Brunei and Singapore.
# Romanization: standard spelling; ng, ny, sy, kh.

@info
romanization|Roman Melayu: a, e, i, o, u; ng (as in sing), ny (as in canyon), sy (sh), kh (kh). Stress usually falls on the last syllable.
